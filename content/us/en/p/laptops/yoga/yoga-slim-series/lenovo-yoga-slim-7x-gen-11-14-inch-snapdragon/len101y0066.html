    What makes the Yoga Slim 7x Gen 11 (14" Snapdragon) different from other laptops?

    The Yoga Slim 7x combines ultraportable design with serious power. At just 13.9mm thin and weighing 1.17kg / 2.58lbs, it features the Snapdragon® X2 Elite processor, all-day battery life, and a stunning 2.8K OLED display. It's built for creators who need consistent performance without being tethered to an outlet.

    What's special about the PureSight Pro display?

    The 14" PureSight Pro OLED display offers 2.8K resolution with 1100nit peak brightness, 100% DCI-P3 and sRGB color gamut, and Dolby Vision® certification. It's DisplayHDR True Black 1000 certified and features TÜV Low Blue Light and Eyesafe® certifications to reduce eye strain during extended sessions.

    Is this laptop good for photo and video editing?

    Absolutely. With the Snapdragon® X2 Elite processor, up to 32GB LPDDR5X memory, and a color-accurate OLED display (99% Adobe RGB), the Yoga Slim 7x handles editing workflows smoothly. The wide color gamut and Delta E<1 accuracy ensure your content looks exactly as intended.

    What ports does the Yoga Slim 7x have?

    The laptop includes three USB 4 ports (two on the left, one on the right), providing versatile connectivity for charging, data transfer, and external displays. These high-speed ports support multiple accessories and peripherals simultaneously.

    Does it come with Windows 11?

    Yes, the Yoga Slim 7x comes with either Windows 11 Home or Windows 11 Pro, depending on your configuration. You'll also get preloaded Lenovo apps, a Microsoft 365 trial, and access to Copilot+ PC features for enhanced productivity.
