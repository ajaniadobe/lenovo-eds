T Series Towers

Power, Unleashed

Lenovo Legion Towers are the pinnacle of engineering, balancing a refined chassis with customizable lighting and elite components. Featuring top-of-the-line processors and discrete graphics, these desktop powerhouses are built to deliver peak performance for gaming, streaming, and professional creation.

View T Series Towers

Whisper-Quiet Performance

These towers are the epitome of staying cool under pressure. Employing an innovative thermal architecture with streamlined air intake (and optional liquid cooling on some models), Lenovo Legion towers keep your CPU and GPU cool even in the most demanding sessions.

Your Power, Your Way

Investing in a Legion Tower means staying ahead of the tech curve. With a tool-free side panel, you gain instant access to your tower’s inner components, so you can future-proof as your ambitions get bigger, whether that means more storage or a better GPU.

Built to Keep You In-Game

Upgrade to Legion Ultimate Support to stay in-game when it matters, with support from specialists who are gamers too. Skip the long wait and get straight to fixing what’s wrong. Remote or on-site, with parts replacement included, you’re covered whenever issues hit.
