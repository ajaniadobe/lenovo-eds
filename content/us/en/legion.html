What is the Lenovo Legion?

Legion is Lenovo’s gaming ecosystem of high-performance gaming laptops, desktop PCs, handheld gaming PCs, monitors, accessories, and software experiences. It is built for gamers, creators, streamers, and power users who want performance-focused devices for gaming, content creation, and demanding PC workloads.

The Legion portfolio includes a variety of form factors:

  • Legion laptops

  • Legion desktops

  • Legion Go handheld gaming PCs

  • Legion accessories: Gaming monitors, keyboards, mice, headsets, backpacks, and compatible setup accessories.

  • Legion Space software for managing games, performance settings, device controls, and connected accessories.

Across Legion PCs and supported devices, available features can include performance-focused processors, discrete graphics, fast memory, SSD storage, high-refresh-rate displays, Legion Coldfront cooling, Lenovo AI Engine+, Legion TrueStrike keyboards, and Lenovo PureSight displays. Since Legion covers multiple product types and configurations, exact features, specifications, and availability vary by product, model, configuration, and region.

How are Legion laptops different from other Lenovo laptops?

Lenovo laptop families are designed for different needs, including business productivity, everyday computing, creative work, education, and gaming. Legion laptops are Lenovo’s dedicated gaming laptops, built for PC gaming and performance-intensive workloads. Legion laptops place greater emphasis on performance-focused processors, high-performance graphics, high-refresh-rate displays, thermal design, gaming-focused keyboards, and performance controls. All Legion laptops come with different tiers of Legion Coldfront cooling, Lenovo AI Engine+, Legion TrueStrike keyboards, Lenovo PureSight displays, and Legion Space software. This gaming-focused design makes Legion laptops well suited for gaming, streaming, content creation, technical applications, and other graphics-intensive workloads.

What is Lenovo Legion Go? How does Legion Go differ from Legion laptop?

Lenovo Legion Go is Lenovo's family of handheld gaming devices, designed to bring PC gaming into more places and more moments. Depending on the model, Legion Go combines a built-in display, integrated or detachable controls, powerful gaming hardware, and Windows or SteamOS.

Compared with a Legion gaming laptop, Legion Go offers a different way to experience PC gaming:

  • Play Anywhere
    Designed for handheld, tabletop, docked, and travel gaming, giving players more opportunities to enjoy their PC game library beyond a traditional desk setup.

  • Purpose-Built for Handheld Gaming
    Features integrated, or detachable game controllers and a gaming-first experience optimized for portable play, while Legion laptops provide a larger display, keyboard, and a more traditional PC setup.

  • Complements Your Gaming Setup
    Legion Go expands when and where you can play, while Legion laptops remain the primary choice for larger-screen gaming, productivity, content creation, and multitasking.

Features, operating systems, performance, controls, and availability vary by model and region.
